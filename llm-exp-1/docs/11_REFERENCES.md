# References and Research Links

**Literature checked through:** 2026-08-08

This bibliography prioritizes directly relevant primary research. Recent arXiv work is explicitly labeled as preprint.

## Moral judgment datasets and benchmarks

### Hendrycks et al. — ETHICS
Dan Hendrycks, Collin Burns, Steven Basart, Andrew Critch, Jerry Li, Dawn Song, Jacob Steinhardt. **Aligning AI With Shared Human Values.** ICLR 2021.

- Repository: https://github.com/hendrycks/ethics

### Lourie, Le Bras, Choi — SCRUPLES
Nicholas Lourie, Ronan Le Bras, Yejin Choi. **SCRUPLES: A Corpus of Community Ethical Judgments on 32,000 Real-Life Anecdotes.** AAAI 2021.

- Paper: https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/17589
- Repository: https://github.com/allenai/scruples
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.09094

### Emelin et al. — Moral Stories
Denis Emelin, Ronan Le Bras, Jena D. Hwang, Maxwell Forbes, Yejin Choi. **Moral Stories: Situated Reasoning about Norms, Intents, Actions, and their Consequences.** EMNLP 2021.

- Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.54/
- Repository: https://github.com/demelin/moral_stories
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2021.emnlp-main.54

### Ziems et al. — NormBank
Caleb Ziems, Jane Dwivedi-Yu, Yi-Chia Wang, Alon Halevy, Diyi Yang. **NormBank: A Knowledge Bank of Situational Social Norms.** ACL 2023.

- Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.429/
- Repository: https://github.com/SALT-NLP/normbank
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2023.acl-long.429

### Russo et al. — Pluralistic Moral Gap / Moral Dilemma Dataset
Giuseppe Russo, Debora Nozza, Paul Röttger, Dirk Hovy. **The Pluralistic Moral Gap: Understanding Moral Judgment and Value Differences between Humans and Large Language Models.** EACL 2026.

- Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.305/
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2026.eacl-long.305

Key reported dataset properties: 1,618 real-world dilemmas with distributions of human binary judgments and free-text rationales.
